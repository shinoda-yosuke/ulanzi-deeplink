## English

**Name:** Deeplink Launcher

**Summary:** Open any deeplink (URL scheme) such as slack://, obsidian://, zoommtg:// or https:// with a key press.

**Detailed introduction:**

```text
Deeplink Launcher adds an "Open Deeplink" action that opens any URL with the app registered for its scheme: app deeplinks such as slack://, obsidian://, zoommtg://, raycast:// or spotify:, web pages (https://), mailto: links and system settings (x-apple.systempreferences:, ms-settings:).

How to use
1. Drag "Open Deeplink" onto a key.
2. Enter the URL in the settings and press "Test" to try it.
3. Press the key to open the link.

- Spaces and non-ASCII characters are percent-encoded automatically.
- If a link cannot be opened (no URL, no scheme or no app registered for it), the key shows an alert and Ulanzi Studio tells you why.
- Works in Multi Actions. macOS and Windows. 8 languages.
- No data is collected or sent anywhere; the URL is only handed to your operating system.

Source code (MIT): https://github.com/shinoda-yosuke/ulanzi-deeplink
```

**Update note (1.0.0):** First release.
